Four pieces of written analysis, each asking you to explain a decision or a
match using that point in the semester's vocabulary — never a multiple-choice
test of champion abilities, item costs or patch notes. Every assessment is
marked on the argument and the evidence behind it; account rank is not part
of any rubric on this page. See [Policies](/policies/) for what match
evidence you're asked to submit and what you're not.
