Twelve weeks, each built around one question rather than one topic. The
first four weeks establish what a match is actually made of — resources,
power, the wave, trading. The middle third moves outward from the individual
lane to information, tempo and objectives at the map level. The last third
puts a whole team on the board — draft, teamfighting, the shifting metagame
— and Week 12 closes the semester by tracing one complete match with every
tool the course has built.

Every week builds on at least one earlier week; none of them is a standalone
topic you could skip and still follow what comes after.
