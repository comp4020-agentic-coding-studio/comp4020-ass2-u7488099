# The Minion Wave

Why do six minions determine what ten champions are allowed to do?

---

## The wave belongs to neither of you

Gold, experience, cooldowns — every other resource in this course belongs
to a player and moves when they do.

The wave has its own rules for where it sits, and those rules decide what
**both** laners are allowed to do while it's between them.

```comment
Opening framing slide. Land the point that the wave is a resource neither

```

---

## Neutral / even

```text
BLUE TOWER |........ o o o|o o o ........| RED TOWER
              (wave meets near the midpoint)
```

Roughly matched kills on both sides. Neither laner is restricted — both can
farm, roam, or fight without giving up ground.

---

## Frozen

```text
BLUE TOWER | o o o|o o o ....................| RED TOWER
         (wave held just outside blue's tower range)
```

Held there deliberately by the side with priority. Blue farms safely under
tower; red cannot extend forward without walking into the wave — and
whatever's behind it.

---

## Slow push

```text
BLUE TOWER |.............. o o o o o o|o ..| RED TOWER
        (blue stops last-hitting; the wave stacks and creeps)
```

One side stops last-hitting. Minions stack up unmatched and the wave grows
larger while it slowly moves toward the other tower.

---

## Crashing

```text
BLUE TOWER |........................ o o o o o o o o| RED TOWER
              (the stacked wave clears fast into red's tower)
```

The stacked wave finally reaches a tower and clears into it quickly. For a
few seconds, nobody needs to be standing in the lane at all.

## What a state buys you

| Wave state | Recall | Roam | Contest an objective |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Neutral | safe | safe | open |
| Frozen (behind it) | safe | risky | open |
| Frozen (in front of it) | risky | punished | closed |
| Slow push (yours, building) | risky | risky | open |
| Crashing | free window | free window | open |

## A frozen lead is a lead that can't leave

Blue is two levels and 800 gold ahead in lane. The wave is frozen just
outside blue's own tower.

Blue's jungler starts a Dragon fight two screens away. Blue cannot help —
not because of anything on the scoreboard, but because leaving now hands
red a wave crashing into blue's tower, unanswered.

The gold lead is real. It is also, for the next ninety seconds, unspendable.
