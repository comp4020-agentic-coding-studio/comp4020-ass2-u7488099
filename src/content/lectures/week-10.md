## Guiding question

> Ten champions enter a fight. What is each one actually trying to
> accomplish?

A teamfight is a bundle of trades (Week 4) happening at once, usually over an
objective (Week 8) — reading it means separating each champion's job from the
chaos of the fight as a whole.
