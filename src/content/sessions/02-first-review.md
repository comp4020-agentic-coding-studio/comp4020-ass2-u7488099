## Before the session

Bring the match and the written answer from Week 1. Reread your own answer
and find the one claim in it you'd have the hardest time defending if
someone asked "how do you know?"

## In the session

Short demos: describe your match's state at one moment — gold, level, wave,
whatever's relevant — in under two minutes, then name the claim you're least
sure about. Feedback is aimed at that claim, not at polishing the summary
around it. This is a rehearsal for the standard the assessments hold you to
from here on: a claim that names a specific, checkable moment beats a
confident generalisation, and neither is stronger for citing your rank
instead of the game state.

## Afterwards

Not assessed. What should change afterward is which moments in your match
you'd reach for as evidence — that's the skill [Solo Queue
Autopsy](/assessments/solo-queue-autopsy/) marks directly, due in Week 4.
