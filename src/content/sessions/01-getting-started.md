## Before the session

Have one complete match ready — yours or one you watched, doesn't matter
which. Watch it again if you need to; you won't get another chance to pick a
different one for Week 1's assessment thread later in the semester.

Write down an answer, in whatever language you currently have, to "why did
the winning team actually win?" `"They got more kills"` or `"their late
game was stronger"` count. What matters is that it's specific to your match,
not a general theory of the game.

## In the session

Short introductions, then Week 1's lecture: why the Nexus is the one
objective nobody can aim at directly, and the chain — resources, advantages,
control, objectives — that the rest of the semester fills in. The last
twenty minutes are yours: in pairs, read each other's "why did they win"
answer and ask one question about it — not to correct it, just to find the
moment underneath the summary.

## Afterwards

Nothing is marked this week. Keep your written answer — Week 12 asks you to
answer the same question about the same match again, and the distance
between the two answers is most of what this course is trying to teach.
